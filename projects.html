BIRTH

"witchtype" is a place for me to consolodate personal interests, research, and unspecific thoughts. i am using it as a space to talk about basically anything i would like: my feelings, ideas, work etc. this is both a personal blog and a place for me to write nondescriptly about many things. my general interests include: computer science, functional programming, philosophy, psychology, neuroscience, cognition, poetry, music, art, and roleplaying games amongst many others. i hope that people here manage to get an idea of who i am as well as what fulfils me. you will find here both more serious structured posts as well as disjointed tangents. if you are interested, you can find out more in about.
